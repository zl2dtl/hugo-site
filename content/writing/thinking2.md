It's a little embarrassing, really. How do you even begin to write about someone when you don't think you've ever quite managed to explain how much they mean to you? It's not as though I don't have the words. I spend a frankly unreasonable amount of time thinking about these things. I just seem to have a rather unfortunate habit of keeping most of them to myself.

I really don't think I tell her enough. I don't think I tell her anywhere near enough, actually. It's become something of a joke at this point, how often I apparently think all these lovely things and how rarely I actually say them. And the funny thing is, it's not that I don't want to. Quite the opposite. Sometimes I think I have so much to say that I don't quite know where to start.

I love her. And I mean that in a way that I don't think I can particularly justify, quantify, or even logically comprehend. It's a rather inconvenient amount of affection to have for one person, if I'm honest. I can sit and think about it for ages, try to work out exactly what it is, why it is, how it is, and eventually come to the conclusion that I'm probably overthinking something that really doesn't need to be thought about at all.

Because that's the other thing. Usually, my head is a rather busy place. Thinking is practically a hobby, although not necessarily one I'd recommend. There's always something to analyse, something to worry about, some little detail to pick apart until I've managed to make it considerably more complicated than it ever needed to be. But when I'm talking to her, something changes. The thinking just sort of stops. Not in a bad way, either. It's peaceful. I can just exist for a bit. I can talk, laugh, make stupid jokes, listen to her talk about whatever happens to be on her mind, and be perfectly happy doing absolutely nothing else.

I don't think she quite understands how much I enjoy that. How something as simple as a conversation with her can make an otherwise entirely ordinary day considerably better. How I can look forward to talking to her, remember some little thing she's said, or find myself wanting to tell her something simply because she's the person I'd most like to tell it to.

And she's just lovely. I really don't know how else to put it. She's so pretty, which is an almost laughably inadequate way of describing it, but there really isn't a better word that comes to mind. And she's so intelligent, too. Not in a way that makes conversation feel like some sort of intellectual competition, or makes you feel as though you need to have a PhD to keep up. She's just effortlessly knowledgeable about so many things. There's always something interesting to hear, something new to learn, or some little detail that I wouldn't have thought of myself. And, of course, whatever she doesn't know, I like to think I can make up for. It seems a reasonably fair arrangement.

I love the little things, too. The jokes, the conversations that go nowhere in particular, the things that probably wouldn't seem especially interesting to anyone else. I love how easy it is to talk to her. I love that I can be completely ridiculous and not feel particularly ridiculous. I love hearing what she thinks about things, even when I disagree, and probably especially when I disagree. I love that there's always something more to discover about her. I don't think I could get bored of listening to her.

There's another thing I don't think I've ever mentioned, which is that I sometimes find myself looking through pictures of us. Not necessarily because I'm looking for anything in particular, or because I've forgotten what happened, but simply because they make me happy. I can look at the same pictures over and over again and somehow they never get old. It's quite ridiculous, really. There are pictures I've probably seen dozens of times, and yet I'll still stop and look at them, remember the moment, and find myself smiling like an idiot. I think there's something quite special about having little reminders of moments that made you happy, and being able to revisit them whenever you like. I don't know. I just really like having those pictures. I like that they're of us. And I like that I get to make more of them.

And perhaps that's what makes it so difficult to explain. It's not one particular thing. It's not a list of qualities that I can neatly put together and point to as evidence. It's all of it. Her being her. The way she talks, the way she thinks, the way she makes me laugh, the way I feel when we're together. It's a thousand little things that individually might seem insignificant, but collectively have somehow become an absolutely enormous part of my life.

I suppose I could keep going. I probably could fill considerably more pages than anyone would reasonably want to read. And that's sort of the point, isn't it? I have all these things to say, and yet somehow, when it comes to actually saying them to her, I manage to make a complete mess of it. It's almost impressive.

So, if you're reading this, I'm sorry that I don't say it enough. I really do mean it when I say I love you. More than I can explain, more than I can reasonably get my head around, and probably considerably more than I manage to communicate.

I hope you know that. And if you don't, I suppose this is me trying to do a slightly better job of making sure you do.
