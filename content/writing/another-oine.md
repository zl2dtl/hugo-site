Apparently I am doing this again.

I said in the last post that I would try not to disappear for another month, and then, rather impressively, I disappeared for another month. I don't really know where September went. It feels like I wrote the last post about five minutes ago, and somehow we're already at the end of the month again.

So, for the three people who actually read this thing, here's what has been happening.

School has been a fairly big part of the last month, mostly because mock exams happened. They're done now, thankfully, and results have started coming back. So far, things seem fine. Nothing has come back that has made me seriously question all of my life choices, which I think counts as a success.

The last month has honestly just been exhausting. It's felt like I've been busy almost constantly, with one thing after another happening and never really getting much of a chance to stop. I spent far too much time being busy, which sounds like a fairly stupid thing to complain about, but I've realised that I really don't like being busy all the time. Doing things is overrated.

The school holidays are here now, so I've got two weeks of mostly doing absolutely nothing before the exams start looming over me again. I'm quite happy with that arrangement. Two weeks where I don't have to constantly think about what I should be doing sounds pretty good right now.

I've also somehow changed phones again.

I sold my iPhone 13 mini. I actually really liked that phone. It was small, fast, had a good camera, and was probably one of the last phones I could use without feeling like I was carrying a small tablet around in my pocket.

And what did I replace it with?

A six-year-old Android phone.

Because apparently that makes sense.

It's a Pixel 5, which came out in 2020, and yet I'm currently running the latest version of Android on it. There is something quite funny about having a phone that is old enough to legally have opinions about modern smartphones while still running current software.

It also works surprisingly well. I'm not sure if this is a sensible decision or just another example of me refusing to buy the thing everyone else is buying. Probably both.

Outside of school, I haven't really been doing much worth writing a massive list about. I've mostly been keeping to myself, not seeing many people, riding the moped and doing whatever I feel like doing when I actually have the time and energy for it.

And honestly, that's been pretty nice.

I've spent enough time being busy that doing nothing has started to feel like an actual activity. Sometimes I don't want to go anywhere or see anyone or start another thing that needs finishing. Sometimes I just want to sit around, go for a ride on the moped, mess around with something for a bit, or waste several hours doing something completely pointless.

The moped is still around too. Thankfully, it actually works now, which is probably for the best considering how much time I've spent on it. It has gone from being this slightly ridiculous project sitting around in pieces to something I can actually use.

I've been riding it quite a bit lately. Sometimes I have somewhere to go, and sometimes I really don't. It's just nice to get out for a while without having to turn it into something productive.

I'm still not entirely convinced it won't decide to break at the worst possible moment, but that's part of the experience at this point. I've accepted that owning it means occasionally wondering whether a strange noise is normal.

It usually isn't.

Oh, and you know the week is going well when the most significant highlight was buying a pair of headphones.

The Technics AZ100s. Yes, they're expensive. Yes, they're absolutely beautiful. Yes, they were completely necessary in my life.

Very necessary.

So necessary, in fact, that shortly afterwards I had to start finding coins on the floor to pay for petrol for the moped.

Financial planning is clearly going very well.

I think the biggest thing about the last month is that I've finally realised how much I don't want to spend all my time doing things. There always seems to be something that needs doing, something I could be working on, somewhere I could be going or something I could be worrying about. After a while, it all gets a bit much.

So now I'm trying to enjoy having a bit of nothing.

I've got two weeks of holidays, the mocks are out of the way, and for once I don't really feel like I need to fill every spare hour with something. I'm sure I'll get bored eventually, and then I'll probably find some completely unnecessary project to occupy myself with, because that's usually how this goes.

But for now, doing nothing sounds pretty good.

And, of course, obligatory daniel talks about his feelings section.

I have been going a little bit insane with all the stress lately, and I still can't quite bring myself to talk about most of it. I'm not even entirely sure why. There are things I've thought about talking about, things I've probably should talk about, and yet somehow I still manage to keep most of it to myself.

In spite of that, and probably in spite of me, she stays around.

I don't think there's a single thing I appreciate more in the world than having someone who will just be there, even when I'm not particularly good at explaining what's going on or even knowing myself what's going on. I don't always make it particularly easy, but she's still there, and that means more to me than I probably know how to put into words.

Maybe the headphones come close, but at this point I'm basically forced to enjoy those after spending that much money on them.

Anywayss. Gotta love her. Pretty brilliant. Something like that. (I really do love her...)


So that's about it. Mocks are done. The grades seem alright. I've sold the iPhone and replaced it with a phone from 2020 because apparently I'm like that. The moped works. I've spent a lot of time being busy and not nearly enough time doing nothing, and now I'm finally getting a couple of weeks where I can just exist for a bit.

Then the exams start again.

Fantastic.

And I've once again managed to neglect the blog.

At least I eventually came back, right.... 

I'll try to update this one a little sooner next time.
