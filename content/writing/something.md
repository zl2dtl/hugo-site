It's Tuesday the 29th of September, 10:35pm, and I sit in bed awake, staring at the ceiling.

Reminiscing on the past, looking into the future, but no further than a year or so away, thinking.

What do I know? What don't I know? What should I know?

Usually, I start thinking like this after something big. Something catastrophic and life-changing that seems to change the fate of my future as I know it.

But not this time.

I like to think of myself as a realist, seeing things for what they are and dealing with them as they come. Often, though, it probably just sounds like pessimism. And maybe, just maybe, it is.

I'm afraid of few things in life. I'll try something new or jump right in, provided my preemptive analysis deems it okay. But there is only one thing I am truly scared of.

Losing her.

And that's the part that scares me most, because I'm not entirely sure if I'm afraid of losing her, or afraid that I already am.

Again, not mine in any sense to keep, but in a connected sense. Or at least I think so. I don't know what I know anymore.

Nothing has led me to think that anything is out of the ordinary, or problematic. There isn't some single event I can point to and say, *there, that's when it changed*. But quite frankly, I have no idea why she stays around for me, and lately I've found myself wondering whether she still wants to.

I feel like I truly don't deserve her, yet I also feel like I need her. At the same time, I feel like I'm one irrational emotional decision away from losing her, despite never actually being told that this is the case at all.

Maybe that's the problem. I'm trying to make sense of something I don't actually have enough information to understand.

So what's the right way to love someone you're not dating?

If I said it every time I thought it, I feel like I would be perceived so fundamentally differently that I wouldn't be myself anymore.

And that leads me back to the reminiscing.

I remember two years ago. It felt different. So different. But not necessarily in a good way.

I think the one point of comparison in my life has constantly been my past self. I've made so many bad decisions. Out of everything comes a lesson, at least, but every lesson has a cost.

I think I dwell too much on things to the point where I can't truly stay in the moment anymore. I'm always looking backwards at what I've done, or forwards at what might happen, and somewhere in between I forget that I'm actually here.

I'm worried.

About my future. About where it will take me. About where it will take *us*.

I know, confidently, that I will make it one way or another. I know that you will too. But I'm worried that somewhere along the way, making it will come at the cost of us.

And maybe that's what I'm really afraid of. Not some distant future where everything falls apart, but that somewhere between now and then, things change quietly enough that I don't notice until it's too late.

We've changed so much. I don't always know what I have to be, what I am, or what specifically I can do. I know things get resolved. I know that things have a way of working themselves out.

But I feel like so much is left unsaid.

And then again, who am I to say any of this?

I'm probably just overthinking after all.

It's now 10:55pm.

Maybe I'm a bit more content with my thoughts. Nothing has really changed. I haven't solved anything. I don't suddenly understand the future, or myself, or us.

But at least I can think clearly about something else now.

Whether this ever makes it onto the blog is something I still haven't decided. I don't know if I'm content with sharing this much uncertainty. Maybe some things are better left as thoughts written at 10:35pm and never shown to anyone.

Or maybe that's exactly why they should be written.

I don't know.

And, for once, I think I'm okay with not knowing.
