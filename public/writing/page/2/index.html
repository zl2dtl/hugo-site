Slowly, Then All at Once

She asked for a post and I had nothing. Which is a first, sort of. Usually there’s something rattling around worth writing about, but today it was just this vague sense that everything is moving both too slowly and too quickly at the same time, and I couldn’t work out how to make that into something worth reading. Then I realised that was exactly the thing worth writing about. I remember when school felt like a drag. My earliest writing, the stuff from two or three laptops ago now, was full of it. It started on an old HP Elitebook, moved to the ThinkPad, then briefly lived on a Dell Latitude I picked up because it was small enough to actually carry to school without thinking about it. I gave the Latitude to my sister when I was done with it, which was the right thing to do, but the backups of everything I’d written went with it. She’s still using it, which is good. The ThinkPad is still sitting around despite being broken, which is less rational but I can’t quite bring myself to throw it out. Too much history in a thing that size. ...

But I’m back, and I had a pretty packed weekend, so let’s get into it. This weekend has been a funny mix of making money and spending it. About a year ago I started making 3D printed morse keys as a little side hustle. Nothing serious, just a way to scrape a bit of cash from the hobby. They were the first thing I ever sold, ever, and there’s something strange and good about making actual money from something you built with your hands. I kept at it for a while then drifted off, as I tend to do. Then out of nowhere this weekend three orders landed at once. Not life changing stuff, about $50 all up, but that’s not really the point.
